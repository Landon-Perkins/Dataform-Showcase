# Portfolio Guide

This repository is structured to communicate a clear message to a hiring manager or senior stakeholder: the author can design a reliable data model architecture, explain the business context behind it, and build the supporting operational controls that make the work trustworthy.

## What this project demonstrates

### Analytics engineering foundations
The repo shows a thoughtful layered design with clearly separated responsibilities between source, staging, and reporting models.

### Business understanding
The domains covered here are not abstract. They reflect real operational and financial concepts such as POS, sell-in, rebates, product hierarchy, and customer membership.

### Data quality and stewardship
The inclusion of assertions, snapshots, and operational logic signals that the author is thinking beyond the happy path.

### Communication quality
The repository is organized in a way that is easy to explain in a walkthrough and easy to understand during a code review.

## How to present it in an interview

A strong interview story would be:

- this project shows clear modeling architecture
- it separates business logic from raw ingestion
- it builds trusted intermediate models
- it includes operational and quality protections
- it is designed to be explainable and maintainable

## What a hiring manager should notice

- the repository reads like a real product, not a demo toy
- the structure signals maturity and tradeoff-aware design
- the project has a clear domain story and model flow
- the author is comfortable translating business logic into data assets
- the repo communicates both technical depth and practical judgment

## Final assessment

This project is strong because it balances realism with clarity. It keeps valuable operational context instead of stripping away the details that make the work credible, while also presenting that context in a much more polished and reviewable format.
